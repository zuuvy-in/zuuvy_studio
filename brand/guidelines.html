Brand guideline · v1 · September 2026

Zuuvy Studio

The AI website builder in the Zuuvy family. It shares Zuuvy's mark, colours and type. It adds one idea: the route ends at a website.

The mark

Zuuvy's route, arriving at a page

The Zuuvy mark is a delivery route: a start box, a road with two stops, and a signal square where the goods arrive. Studio keeps all of it. The destination becomes a page, shown as the signal square with a browser bar. Nothing else changes, so the two marks read as one family at any size.

  • Start box

    Where a business begins. Outlined, never filled.

  • The road and its two stops

    11-unit stroke on a 100-unit grid, mitred corners, square ends. The stops are gaps cut at the first two turns.

  • Destination page · Studio only

    The signal square grows to 21 units and carries a white bar, 13 × 2.6, which reads as a browser toolbar. This is the only difference from the Zuuvy mark.

Clear space

Keep one start-box width (16 units) free on every side. Nothing enters the dashed area.

Minimum sizes

The browser bar disappears below 24 px. That is fine: at 16 px the mark is Zuuvy's, which is correct.

Lockups

Mark, then the word

“Zuuvy” is Space Grotesk Bold and “Studio” is Space Grotesk Regular, at the same size and on one baseline. Size a lockup by its height. The mark sits the full cap height plus the descender.

Zuuvy Studio
Zuuvy Studio

Do

  • Use the ink lockup on white or paper, and the white lockup on ink.
  • Keep the signal square red on every background, including ink.
  • Use the tile (mark on an ink square) as the app icon and favicon.

Don't

  • Recolour the signal square, or put the lockup on a signal-red field.
  • Rotate the mark, outline it, add a shadow, or rebuild it in another stroke weight.
  • Write “Zuuvy Sites Studio” or “ZuuvyStudio”. The name is two words: Zuuvy Studio.

Ink carries the product. Signal marks the one thing that matters.

Ink and Signal are Zuuvy's own colours, the same values the till and HQ use. Studio adds one colour, AI violet, and uses it only where the AI is working.

Product rules

What the brand promises, as behaviour

Nothing goes live by surprise

Every change, human or AI, is a version. Publishing is always a separate, named press.

Your decisions are fixed

Brand-kit values and existing content are never changed by the AI without asking. It proposes, and you allow.

You can always go back

Every version is kept. You can restore the layout, and any content table, from any version.

The AI is visible

AI work shows in violet, with the prompt and the model recorded on the version.
